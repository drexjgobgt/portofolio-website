The city
is the client.

SIPW is a public complaint system for Medan’s city agencies. Residents drop a pin, attach a photo, and follow the report from verification to completion. Behind it, an AI triage layer routes each complaint to the right office — and gets out of the way when it isn’t sure.

9 complaint categories mapped to city agencies
60% confidence guardrail before a human reviews
SLA tracked from report to resolution

The AI is allowed to be unsure. The report is not allowed to be lost.

Built with React, Node, PostgreSQL, Leaflet, Recharts, and a DeepSeek-based classification layer.

The AI can be offline. The report still saves.
